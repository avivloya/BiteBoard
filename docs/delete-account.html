Snapspoon

Delete your Snapspoon account

Google Play and the App Store let you delete your account from inside the app or from this page.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Snapspoon and go to Account.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed in with (or tell us your @username) and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username, picture and bio
  • Your posts, recipes, photos and videos
  • Your comments, likes, follows and messages
  • Your food log, weigh-ins and goals backed up to your account

Crash reports stay, with your account removed from them. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google as the law requires. To stop a subscription, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings; deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

See also the privacy policy.
