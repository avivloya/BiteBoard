Using Biteboard

By using Biteboard you agree to these terms and our privacy policy. You must be at least 13, and under 18 with a parent or guardian's OK. Keep your account secure and don't share it.

What you can't post

  • Spam, scams or fake accounts, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Nudity or sexual content.
  • Bullying, harassment, hate speech, threats or violence.
  • Unsafe weight-loss content, like eating under 1,200 kcal a day, fasting for days, diet pills, or anything that promotes eating disorders.
  • Made-up nutrition facts presented as real.
  • Music, videos or photos you don't have the rights to. Use the free Biteboard sounds.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules. You can report anything with ⋯ or Report, and block anyone.

Your content

You own what you post. By posting it publicly, you let Biteboard show, store and share it inside the app and in links people share, for as long as it's posted. Delete it and we stop showing it.

Not medical advice

Calorie estimates, AI recipes and Bitey's answers are best guesses and can be wrong. Check with a doctor before big changes to your diet, especially if you're under 18, pregnant, or have a health condition.

Paying for Verified doesn't guarantee the badge: it turns on only after your ID is checked, and it can be removed for breaking the verification rules in Settings.

You can delete your account any time in Settings. We may close accounts that seriously or repeatedly break these terms.
