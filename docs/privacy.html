Snapspoon

Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version: your recipe page, posts, videos, comments, likes and who you follow are public. Your weight, food log and goals stay private on your phone. We don't sell your data or show you ads based on it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email or sign-in, username, name, bio and profile picture.
  • What you post: recipes, photos, videos, captions, words on videos, your chosen sound and cover, comments and replies.
  • What you do with others: who you follow, your likes, your messages, the people you block and the reports you send.
  • Your health and goals: age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity, food log and weight log. These stay on your phone (or in your browser on the website) and are not sent to our servers.
  • Your phone: a push notification token if you turn notifications on, and basic crash information.

Who can see what

  • Public: your profile, posts, videos, recipes you share, comments, likes, who you follow and who follows you.
  • Private: your messages, which only you and the person you're talking to can read. If a message is reported, Snapspoon can review it to keep people safe.
  • Only you: your weight, food log, goals, saved boards and the people you block.

Services that help run Snapspoon

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts, videos and messages.
  • Anthropic (Claude) estimates calories from meal photos, writes AI recipes and powers Bitey. We send only the photo or question you ask about, and only to answer it.
  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode number when you scan a package.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle subscriptions. We never see your card details.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.

Verification

If you apply for the verified badge, we use the proof you send (like a photo ID) only to check who you are, and we delete it within 30 days of our decision.

Your choices

  • Change your profile any time in Account › Edit profile.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications.
  • Export your data in Settings › Your data › Export my data.
  • Delete your account in Settings › Your data › Delete account. This removes your profile, posts, videos, comments, likes, follows and messages from our servers.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Snapspoon. If you're under 18, use it with a parent or guardian's OK. Weight-loss advice in Snapspoon is general information, not medical advice.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app for big changes. Questions? Message @snapspoon in the app.
